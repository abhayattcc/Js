
COMPUTER

1.  A computer is an electronic device that performs calculations and operations based on instructions provided by a __________.
    a) Software or hardware program
    b) Human operator
    c) Mechanical system
    d) Electrical circuit
    Answer: a) Software or hardware program

2.  Which of the following is a characteristic of computers, indicating their ability to process millions of calculations per second?
    a) Accuracy
    b) Diligence
    c) Speed
    d) Versatility
    Answer: c) Speed

3.  Computers are highly accurate because they work on:
    a) Human instructions
    b) Inbuilt software programs
    c) Mechanical parts
    d) Electrical impulses
    Answer: b) Inbuilt software programs

4.  The characteristic of computers that refers to their ability to perform complex and long calculations with the same speed and accuracy is:
    a) Versatility
    b) Storage
    c) Diligence
    d) Speed
    Answer: c) Diligence

5.  What characteristic allows computers to perform various operations at the same time?
    a) Speed
    b) Accuracy
    c) Versatility
    d) Storage
    Answer: c) Versatility

6.  The ability of computers to store a large amount of data or instructions in its memory is known as:

TYPES OF COMPUTER

1.  Which type of computer stores data continuously in the form of physical quantities and produces output as a graph?
    a) Digital Computer
    b) Hybrid Computer
    c) Analog Computer
    d) Supercomputer
    Answer: c) Analog Computer

2.  Analog computers are used in the calculation of physical variables such as:
    a) Binary numbers
    b) Discrete values
    c) Voltage, pressure, temperature, speed
    d) Logical operations
    Answer: c) Voltage, pressure, temperature, speed

3.  Which type of computer works with data represented in binary 0s and 1s?
    a) Analog Computer
    b) Digital Computer
    c) Hybrid Computer
    d) Mainframe Computer
    Answer: b) Digital Computer

4.  Digital computers are extensively used for solving complex problems in the field of:
    a) Weather forecasting only
    b) Engineering & technology, design, research and data processing
    c) Controlling physical variables
    d) Simple calculations
    Answer: b) Engineering & technology, design, research and data processing

5.  A computer that combines features of Analog and Digital computers is called a:
    a) Micro Computer
    b) Mini Computer
    c) Hybrid Computer
    d) Supercomputer
    Answer: c) Hybrid Computer

6.  In large industries and businesses, a hybrid computer can be used for:
    a) Only logical operations
    b) Only efficient processing of differential equations
    c) Logical operations as well as efficient processing of differential equations
    d) Playing games and surfing the Internet
    Answer: c) Logical operations as well as efficient processing of differential equations

7.  Which type of computer is small, inexpensive, and commonly used at homes for playing games and surfing the Internet?
    a) Mini Computer
    b) Mainframe Computer
    c) Micro Computer
    d) Supercomputer
    Answer: c) Micro Computer

8.  Computers that possess most of the features and capabilities of a large computer but are smaller in physical size are known as:
    a) Micro Computer
    b) Mini Computer
    c) Mainframe Computer
    d) Supercomputer
    Answer: b) Mini Computer

9.  Minicomputers are typically used for:
    a) Personal use at home
    b) Small or mid-range operating business and scientific applications
    c) Large-scale applications supporting hundreds of users
    d) Weather forecasting and fluid dynamics
    Answer: b) Small or mid-range operating business and scientific applications

10. Which type of computer is expensive, large, and capable of supporting hundreds of users simultaneously?
    a) Micro Computer
    b) Mini Computer
    c) Mainframe Computer
    d) Supercomputer
    Answer: c) Mainframe Computer

11. Mainframe computers are used for:
    a) Personal entertainment
    b) Solving specific problems with pre-programmed instructions
    c) Specific large-scale applications
    d) Mobile computing
    Answer: c) Specific large-scale applications

12. Which type of computer is powerful, expensive, and the fastest, capable of performing billions and trillions of calculations per second?
    a) Mainframe Computer
    b) Hybrid Computer
    c) Supercomputer
    d) Workstation
    Answer: c) Supercomputer

13. Supercomputers use architectural and operational principles from:
    a) Sequential processing

14. Supercomputers are used for applications that require large amounts of mathematical computations, such as:
